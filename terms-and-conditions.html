Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026  |  Operated by: Kayla Mayotech LLP

1. Introduction

Welcome to ToolRent, a mobile and digital rental management solution operated by Kayla Mayotech LLP (“we”, “our”, or “us”). ToolRent enables equipment rental businesses, tool shop owners, and contractors to efficiently manage tool inventories, track tool checkouts and returns, process rental agreements, verify customer identity, and administer shop staff.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal and business information when you use our mobile application and related web services. By installing, accessing, or using ToolRent, you agree to the collection and handling of your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide equipment inventory, rental tracking, and staff management features, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Shop & Account Information: Shop owner name, business/shop name, registered mobile phone number, optional business email address, shop address/location, and optional shop profile images.
  • Staff & Employee Details: Employee ID, full name, contact phone number, residential address, profile photographs, and permissions assigned to manage shop operations.
  • Customer & Client Records: Customer names, contact numbers, addresses, active rental count, total due balances, and payment settlement records created by your shop.
  • Identity Verification Documents: Government-issued identity details and document files (such as Aadhaar number, PAN, Driving License, or Voter ID cards, including document photos or PDF files) uploaded to verify customer authenticity, prevent equipment loss or fraud, and formalize rental agreements.
  • Tool & Equipment Inventory Data: Tool titles, codes/serial numbers, equipment categories, tool photos, total stock, available stock, maintenance schedules, and hourly or daily rental tariff rates.
  • Rental Transactions & Agreement Logs: Issued tools, rental start/due return dates, settlement history, overdue flags, and terms associated with rental checkouts.
  • Authentication Data: Mobile phone numbers and OTP (One-Time Password) codes used for secure SMS-based account sign-in.
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and crash/performance logs used to troubleshoot bugs and improve app reliability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following business and operational purposes:

  • To organize and maintain your shop’s machinery, tools, and equipment inventory.
  • To record and track active rentals, returns, overdue items, and equipment availability in real time.
  • To maintain customer profiles and verify customer identity through uploaded documents to secure your rental assets against unauthorized use or theft.
  • To calculate hourly and daily rental charges, compute customer dues, and maintain accurate settlement records.
  • To facilitate multi-shop administration and assign staff/employee access within a shop.
  • To authenticate users via secure SMS OTP login.
  • To provide customer support, resolve technical issues, and roll out feature updates.
  • To comply with applicable Indian statutory, commercial, and financial record-keeping laws.

4. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third-party marketing companies or advertisers.

Your information is only disclosed under strict conditions to the following parties:

  • Cloud Storage & Database Providers: Secure cloud hosting and infrastructure services (such as Google Cloud / Firebase Firestore) to store your encrypted business database, customer records, and uploaded photos.
  • Telecommunication & SMS Gateways: Third-party communication providers to dispatch SMS verification codes (OTPs) and service updates to your phone.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: When required by law, court order, governmental inquiry, or to enforce our rental platform policies and safeguard against fraud.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your shop, customer, tool, and rental records for as long as your ToolRent account remains active and as required to fulfill our operational and tax obligations.

If you wish to terminate your account and remove your stored business records, you can submit a deletion request from the Profile section of the app or by contacting us at support@kaylamayotech.com. Upon verification, your account credentials and personal data will be deleted or anonymized within 30 days, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

6. Your Rights and Controls

As a registered user, you have the right to:

  • Review, update, or edit your personal profile, employee records, shop details, and inventory listings at any time directly through the app interface.
  • Delete specific inventory items, employee profiles, or completed rental logs.
  • Request a copy of your personal data stored within our systems.
  • Withdraw consent and request account closure.

7. Security Safeguards

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational measures in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Information) Rules, 2011.

All communication between the ToolRent mobile application and our backend servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Document uploads and user records are stored in access-controlled environments. While we take every reasonable measure to protect your data, no method of digital transmission or storage is completely impenetrable.

8. Children’s Privacy

ToolRent is a business application designed for shop owners, commercial tool suppliers, and legal adults aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from minors. If we discover that a person under 18 has provided us with personal information, we will take immediate steps to delete such records.

9. Third-Party Analytics & Cookies

Our mobile app does not use third-party behavioral tracking or advertising cookies. We only employ diagnostic analytics (such as Firebase Crashlytics) strictly to identify application errors, monitor uptime, and maintain app stability.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our app features or regulatory requirements. Material revisions will be notified through an in-app update banner or via SMS. Continued use of ToolRent after updates are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
