ToolRent Account Deletion

Application: ToolRent  |  Operated by: Kayla Mayotech LLP

You can request deletion of your ToolRent account and associated personal data using the methods below. This page explains how to submit a request and what happens to information associated with your account.

1. How to find the Delete Account option in the app

  1. Open the ToolRent application.
  2. Log in using your registered mobile number and OTP verification.
  3. After successful login, you will be taken to the Dashboard.
  4. From the Dashboard, open the Profile section.
  5. In the Profile section, select Delete Account and follow the on-screen instructions to submit and confirm your request.

Before confirming: Review the deletion confirmation carefully. Account deletion may affect access to your profile and associated shop information.

If you cannot find the Delete Account option or cannot access your account, submit a request by email using the instructions below.

2. Request deletion by email

Email our support team from the email address associated with your account, if you have one. Include your registered mobile number with country code and state that you want your ToolRent account deleted. Do not send OTPs, passwords, or unnecessary identity documents.

Email an account deletion request

Email: support@kaylamayotech.com

We may contact you to verify that you control the account before processing the request.

3. What data may be deleted

After we verify your request, we will delete or anonymize account-related personal data where applicable, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, and legitimate business record-keeping requirements. This may include:

  • Account and profile information, such as your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Shop profile information associated with your account, where eligible for deletion.
  • Employee profile information associated with the account, where applicable.

4. Data that may be retained

Some records may need to be retained for a limited period where required by applicable law or necessary for legitimate purposes, such as financial and tax records, transaction or rental history, security records, audit logs, or the resolution of disputes and prevention of fraud. Where retention is required, the retained data will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements and our Privacy Policy.

Because ToolRent supports rental businesses and multi-shop accounts, deleting an account may affect access to shop records and operational history. Please ensure you have exported or otherwise preserved any information you are legally entitled to keep before requesting deletion.

5. Processing time

After verification, we aim to complete deletion or anonymization of eligible personal data within 30 days. If some information must be retained, it will be retained only as permitted or required by applicable requirements.
